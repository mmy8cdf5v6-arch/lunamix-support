How do I choose a world?

The start screen shows three big picture cards: funny friends, outfits and monsters. Tap one to open that world. The round home button in the top-left corner of every world brings you back to the start screen.

How do I mix characters?

Swipe the head, the body or the legs left or right; each part moves on its own. In the funny friends world, tap a collection to include or exclude it (at least one stays on). In the outfits world the background colour changes with every swipe. In the monsters world, the buttons on the picture add wings or change the background.

Where are saved pictures?

Save adds the picture to Photos after permission is granted. If saving was denied, allow Lunamix Little to add photos in iOS Settings. The app does not read your other photos.

How do I turn music on or off?

Tap the music-note button in a world. Music starts off and stops when the app goes into the background. Check the device volume and silent mode if you hear nothing.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything is in the app; it needs no internet connection after installation. A sharing destination may need one.

Are there extra purchases?

No. One purchase includes all three worlds. There are no ads, subscriptions, locked packs or in-app purchases.

Why does a question appear?

Save, Share and the Parents areas ask a grown-up to answer a multiplication question. Enter the answer and tap Continue, or choose Back to play. Each time a new question is asked.

Which languages does the app speak?

English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese and Korean. The app follows the device language; other languages show English.
