Lunamix Big Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026.

About this policy

Lunamix Big is provided by Tolga Yeniyurt. This policy explains the information handled when you use the app or contact us for support.

Information in the app

Lunamix Big collects no data. It does not require an account and makes no internet connections of its own: nothing about gameplay, the device or creations is sent to us or to anyone else, and the app contains no advertising or analytics service. It does not request your name, birth date, location, camera, microphone or contacts.

The artwork, music and sounds of all three worlds (fashion, dinosaurs and football) are included with the app and work offline. The music button does not stream from a music service.

What stays on the device

The app remembers, on the device only: the last world opened, each world's current character and settings (for example the chosen parts, colours and backdrop), the music and tap-sound settings, and, in the dinosaur and football worlds, the date Apple's rating window was last shown. That window can appear only there, only after a grown-up has saved or shared a picture behind the grown-up question, and at most once in 60 days; the fashion world never shows it. None of it leaves the device. A grown-up can clear it in the Parents area on the start screen.

Saving and sharing creations

When you choose Save, Lunamix Big asks permission to add a generated picture to Photos. It does not ask to browse or read your existing photos.

When you choose Share, the app writes the generated picture to its temporary storage and opens the system share sheet. You choose the destination; the receiving app or service handles the picture under its own privacy practices. Nothing is uploaded to us.

Purchases

Apple handles the store purchase. Lunamix Big does not collect payment card or bank details and has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Children

Lunamix Big is made for children aged 6 to 8 and their families. The app does not ask children for personal information and has no accounts, chat or public creation feed. A new grown-up question protects Save, Share, the Parents areas and the links to this page and to the support page. Parents should handle support requests and supervise sharing to other services.

Support

If a parent or guardian contacts tolgayeniyurt@gmail.com, the email address and message are used only to answer the request. Please do not include personal information about a child.

Contact and updates

For questions about privacy, contact tolgayeniyurt@gmail.com. Publisher: Tolga Yeniyurt.
