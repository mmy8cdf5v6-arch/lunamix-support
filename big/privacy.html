Lunamix Big Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026.

About this policy

Lunamix Big (shown in the app as Lunamix: Mix&Match) is provided by Tolga Yeniyurt. This policy covers the app on the Apple App Store (iPhone and iPad) and on Google Play (Android), and explains the information handled when you use the app or contact us for support.

Information in the app

Lunamix Big collects no personal data. It does not require an account, and nothing about gameplay, the device or creations is sent to us or to anyone else. There is no Lunamix server. The app contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting service and no tracking, and it does not use an advertising identifier. It does not request your name, birth date, location, camera, microphone or contacts.

The artwork, music and sounds of all the worlds are included with the app and work offline. The music button does not stream from a music service. The app uses the internet only for the store purchase system (Apple App Store or Google Play), to show, buy and restore in-app purchases. On Android the store libraries also check whether a network is available; this is not sent to us.

What stays on the device

The app remembers, on the device only: the last world opened, each world's current character and settings (for example the chosen parts, colours and backdrop), the music and tap-sound settings, and, which worlds have been unlocked by a purchase, so they open offline. A Rate us button in the Parents area, behind the grown-up question, opens the store's own rating window or the app's store page; nothing is unlocked for rating. None of this is sent to us. On Android the app's data is excluded from Android backup. A grown-up can clear it in the Parents area on the start screen.

Saving and sharing creations

When you choose Save, Lunamix Big adds a generated picture to Photos (on iPhone and iPad, after asking permission to add photos) or to the Pictures/Lunamix folder (on Android; older Android versions ask only for permission to write it). It does not browse or read your existing photos.

When you choose Share, the app writes the generated picture to its temporary storage and opens the system share sheet. You choose the destination; the receiving app or service handles the picture under its own privacy practices. Nothing is uploaded to us.

Purchases

Some worlds are free. The other worlds can be unlocked with one-time in-app purchases, one world at a time or as a bundle. There are no subscriptions. Purchases are made and processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive payment card or bank details, and no receipt is sent to us; the app checks the purchase with the store on the device. Restore Purchases unlocks again what the signed-in store account already owns.

The purchase screens and Restore Purchases are behind the grown-up question, so a child cannot start a purchase alone. The store may also ask for its own approval, for example Ask to Buy or Google Play parental approval.

Children

Lunamix Big is made for children aged 6 to 8 and their families. The app does not ask children for personal information and has no accounts, chat or public creation feed. It contains no advertising and no tracking. A new grown-up question protects purchases, Restore Purchases, Save, Share, the Parents areas, Rate us and the links to this page and to the support page. We aim to follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Apple's Kids Category rules and Google Play's Families Policy. Parents should handle support requests and supervise sharing to other services.

Support

If a parent or guardian contacts tolgayeniyurt@gmail.com, the email address and message are used only to answer the request. Please do not include personal information about a child.

Contact and updates

For questions about privacy, contact tolgayeniyurt@gmail.com. Publisher: Tolga Yeniyurt.
