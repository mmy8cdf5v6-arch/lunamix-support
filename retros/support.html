How do I choose a world?

The start screen shows three big tin cards: heroes, robots and rockets. Tap one to open that world. The round home button in the top-left corner of every world brings you back to the start screen.

How do I mix characters?

Swipe the head, the body or the legs left or right; each part moves on its own. In the heroes world, the cape button puts a cape on and the background button changes the backdrop; every hero gets a name. In the robots world, tap or turn the wind-up key and the robot walks across the stage; tap the robot's body and its lamps light up; every robot gets a name. In the rockets world, press the round Fire button: the countdown runs from ten, the rocket blasts off, jets fly past and the rocket lands again.

What are gold characters and trophies?

Now and then a swipe turns up a rare gold character. Each world keeps a showcase of 12 different gold finds; a full showcase wins a trophy, and the showcase starts again. Tap a gold character in the showcase to bring it back. Nothing can be bought.

Where are saved pictures?

Only the heroes world saves pictures. Save adds the picture to Photos after permission is granted. If saving was denied, allow Lunamix Retros to add photos in iOS Settings. The app does not read your other photos.

How do I turn sound on or off?

In the heroes world, the music button turns the chiptune music on or off; music starts off. Tap sounds and the sound effects of each world can be switched off in the Parents area on the start screen. Sound stops when the app goes into the background. Check the device volume and silent mode if you hear nothing.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything is in the app; it needs no internet connection after installation. A sharing destination may need one.

Are there extra purchases?

No. One purchase includes all three worlds. There are no ads, subscriptions, locked packs or in-app purchases.

Why does a question appear?

Save, Share and the Parents areas ask a grown-up to answer a multiplication question. Enter the answer and tap Continue, or choose Back to play. Each time a new question is asked.

Which languages does the app speak?

The start screen and the robots and rockets worlds follow the device language: English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese and Korean; other languages show English. The heroes world is in English.
