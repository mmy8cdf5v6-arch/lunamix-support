Lunamix Retros Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026.

About this policy

Lunamix Retros is provided by Tolga Yeniyurt. This policy explains the information handled when you use the app or contact us for support.

Information in the app

Lunamix Retros collects no data. It does not require an account and makes no internet connections of its own: nothing about gameplay, the device or creations is sent to us or to anyone else, and the app contains no advertising or analytics service. It does not request your name, birth date, location, camera, microphone or contacts.

The artwork, music and sounds of all three worlds (heroes, robots and rockets) are included with the app and work offline. The music button in the heroes world does not stream from a music service; the robot and rocket sounds are made in code on the device.

What stays on the device

The app remembers, on the device only: the last world opened, the current hero and its settings (parts, cape, background and colours), the current robot, the gold characters found and the trophies won in each world, the sound settings, and the dates the opening guides were last shown. None of it leaves the device. A grown-up can clear the saved characters, gold finds and trophies of all three worlds in the Parents area on the start screen.

Saving and sharing creations

Only the heroes world can save and share. When you choose Save, Lunamix Retros asks permission to add a generated picture of the hero to Photos. It does not ask to browse or read your existing photos.

When you choose Share, the app writes the generated picture to its temporary storage and opens the system share sheet. You choose the destination; the receiving app or service handles the picture under its own privacy practices. Nothing is uploaded to us.

Purchases

Apple handles the store purchase. Lunamix Retros does not collect payment card or bank details and has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Children

Lunamix Retros is made for children aged 6 to 8 and their families. The app does not ask children for personal information and has no accounts, chat or public creation feed. A new grown-up question protects Save, Share, the Parents areas and the links to this page and to the support page. Parents should handle support requests and supervise sharing to other services.

Support

If a parent or guardian contacts tolgayeniyurt@gmail.com, the email address and message are used only to answer the request. Please do not include personal information about a child.

Contact and updates

For questions about privacy, contact tolgayeniyurt@gmail.com. Publisher: Tolga Yeniyurt.
