Lunamix Retrobots Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026.

In short

Lunamix Retrobots collects no data and shares no data. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no analytics, and it makes no network connections. On Android it requests no permissions at all, not even internet access.

About this policy

Lunamix Retrobots is provided by Tolga Yeniyurt. This policy explains the information handled when you use the app or contact us for support.

Information in the app

Lunamix Retrobots does not require an account. The app cannot connect to the internet: the robot parts, the pictures and the game itself are included with the app, and on Android the internet permission is removed. It does not send gameplay, identifiers or creations to a developer-operated server and contains no advertising or analytics service. It does not request your name, birth date, location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts.

The sound effects are made on the device while you play. The app has no music and no recorded sound files.

What the app remembers on your device

A few game settings are kept in the app's own storage on the device, so they are still there the next time you open the app: the robot on screen (its head, body and legs), the background choice and whether the colors follow the head; the date the robot of the day was last shown, so the short welcome guide appears only on the first visit of each day; the gold collection (which gold robots were found in the current round, with their head, body and legs; how many rounds have been completed; and which trophies were won); and the Sounds setting from the Parents section.

These values contain no personal information and the app never sends them anywhere. They are deleted when the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared in the device settings. If the device's own Android backup is turned on, the system may include the app's storage in the backup it keeps for the device owner; the app itself does not send anything.

Saving, sharing and links

The app has no Save or Share function and does not read or add pictures on the device. Only in the Parents section, after a grown-up has answered a multiplication question, the app shows the Sounds switch and a link to this policy; the link opens in the device's browser. The app shows no other links, no rating requests and no advertising.

Purchases

The app store handles the purchase of the app. Lunamix Retrobots does not collect payment card or bank details and does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Children

Lunamix Retrobots is intended for children and families. The app does not request personal information from children or operate accounts, chat or a public creation feed. A fresh grown-up question protects the Parents section. Parents should handle support requests.

Support

If a parent or guardian contacts tolgayeniyurt@gmail.com, the email address and message are used only to respond to the request. Do not include personal information about a child.

Contact and updates

For questions about privacy, contact tolgayeniyurt@gmail.com. Publisher: Tolga Yeniyurt.
