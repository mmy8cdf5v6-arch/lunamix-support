How do I make a player?

Swipe the head, the shirt or the legs left or right. Each part moves on its own, so any head can meet any shirt and any legs. The whole kit takes on the colours of the part you swipe last, and the crowd in the stand wears the same colours. Every player gets a name, sometimes a nickname, a club and a position, shown under the picture.

Are the players and clubs real?

No. Every player, nickname, club, crest, kit and advertising board in Lunamix FC is made up. Any likeness to a real person or club is a coincidence. The app is not affiliated with any real club, league, competition or player.

Where are saved pictures?

Save makes a retro trading card of your player. On iPhone and iPad, Save adds the card to Photos after permission is granted. If saving is denied, review the Photos permission of Lunamix FC in iOS Settings. On supported Android devices, Save adds the card to the Pictures/Lunamix FC collection. The app does not scan or read other pictures on the device.

How do I turn the crowd sound on or off?

Tap the button with the cheering fans. The sound starts off, plays real stadium crowd recordings in turn and stops when the app goes into the background. Check device volume and silent-mode settings if necessary.

Does it work offline?

The game and the sound are bundled in the app. The app itself does not need an internet connection after installation. A sharing destination may need one.

Are there extra purchases?

No. One upfront purchase includes all 50 players. There are no ads, subscriptions, locked packs or in-app purchases.

Why does a question appear?

Save, Share and Parents ask a grown-up to answer a multiplication question.

Why does the phone stay upright?

The players are tall, so on phones the app stays in portrait to keep them large. On iPad and Android tablets you can rotate freely.
