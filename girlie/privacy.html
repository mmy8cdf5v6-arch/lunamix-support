Lunamix Girlie Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026.

About this policy

Lunamix Girlie is provided by Tolga Yeniyurt. This policy explains the information handled when you use the app or contact us for support.

Information in the app

The app does not require an account and does not send gameplay, identifiers or creations to a developer-operated server. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting service. It does not request your name, birth date, location, camera, microphone or contacts. All artwork and music are bundled for offline play.

The current combination and background colour are stored in local preferences. Up to 30 legacy saved combinations may also remain from earlier development versions; the current app has no favourites library. These are selections of character parts, not personal profiles. We do not operate cloud synchronization or remote storage. iOS may include app preferences in operating-system backups according to your device settings; Android app backup and transfer are disabled.

Saving and sharing creations

Save and Share require a fresh grown-up question. On iPhone and iPad, Save requests permission only to add the generated picture to Photos, not to browse or read the existing library. On Android 10 and later it saves to Pictures/Girlie; on older Android versions a system document picker lets the grown-up choose the destination.

Share creates a PNG or SVG in the app's local cache and opens the system share sheet. The grown-up chooses the destination; the receiving app or service handles the picture under its own privacy practices. At most two fixed-name export files are retained in the app cache. They are not uploaded to us. Generated files contain the character, not the challenge answer or device identifiers.

Clearing local data

The Parents area's clear action resets saved combinations and the background and attempts to delete cached export files. It does not remove pictures already saved to Photos, documents or another service. Uninstalling removes app-local data, subject to the operating system's backup and restore behaviour. The grown-up answer is processed in memory and is not saved or transmitted.

Children

Girlie belongs to the younger-child Lunamix editions, alongside Kids and Monsters. It has no accounts, chat, public creation feed, tracking or requests for children's personal information. A grown-up question protects Parents, Save and Share; it is an access gate, not identity verification or legal parental consent. Parents should handle support requests and supervise sharing.

Support and updates

Parents or guardians can contact tolgayeniyurt@gmail.com. An email address and message supplied for support are used to respond to the request. Do not include personal information about a child. Publisher: Tolga Yeniyurt. Contact the same address with privacy questions.
