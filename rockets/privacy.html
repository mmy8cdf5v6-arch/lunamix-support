Lunamix Rockets Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026.

In short

Lunamix Rockets collects no data and shares no data. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no analytics, and it makes no network connections. On Android it requests no permissions at all, not even internet access. On iPhone and iPad it also requests no permissions, collects no data and keeps its settings only on the device.

About this policy

Lunamix Rockets is provided by Tolga Yeniyurt. This policy explains the information handled when you use the app or contact us for support.

Information in the app

Lunamix Rockets does not require an account. The app cannot connect to the internet: the rocket parts, the pictures and the game itself are included with the app, and on Android the internet permission is removed. It does not send gameplay, identifiers or creations to a developer-operated server and contains no advertising or analytics service. It does not request your name, birth date, location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts.

The sound effects and the countdown voice are made on the device while you play. The app has no music and no recorded sound files.

What the app remembers on your device

A few game settings are kept in the app's own storage on the device, so they are still there the next time you open the app: whether the colors follow the part you change, and which part sets them; the date the short welcome guide was last shown, so it appears only on the first visit of each day; the gold collection (which gold rockets were found in the current round, with their nose, body and tail; how many gold rockets have been found in total; and how many medals were won); and the Sounds setting from the Parents section. The rocket on screen and the background are not stored.

These values contain no personal information and the app never sends them anywhere. They are deleted when the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared in the device settings. If the device's own Android backup is turned on, the system may include the app's storage in the backup it keeps for the device owner; the app itself does not send anything. On iPhone and iPad the same settings stay in the app's storage on the device; an iCloud or computer backup made by the device owner may include them, and the app itself does not send anything.

Saving, sharing and links

The app has no Save or Share function and does not read or add pictures on the device. Only in the Parents section, after a grown-up has answered a multiplication question, the app shows the Sounds switch and a link to this policy; the link opens in the device's browser. The app shows no other links, no rating requests and no advertising.

Purchases

The app store handles the purchase of the app. Lunamix Rockets does not collect payment card or bank details and does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Children

Lunamix Rockets is intended for children and families. The app does not request personal information from children or operate accounts, chat or a public creation feed. A fresh grown-up question protects the Parents section. Parents should handle support requests.

Support

If a parent or guardian contacts tolgayeniyurt@gmail.com, the email address and message are used only to respond to the request. Do not include personal information about a child.

Contact and updates

For questions about privacy, contact tolgayeniyurt@gmail.com. Publisher: Tolga Yeniyurt.
