How do I build a rocket?

Swipe the nose, the body or the tail left or right. Each part moves on its own, so any nose can meet any body and any tail.

What does the big red button do?

Press it to launch. A robot voice counts down from ten to zero while the rocket shakes on the pad, then it lifts off in flame and smoke and flies out of the top of the screen. Three copies of your rocket zoom across the sky, and the rocket comes back down slowly and lands where it started. The whole launch takes about 24 seconds; the parts and the button rest until it has landed.

What happens when I tap a part?

Tap the nose and its windows light up while it plays a little tune. Tap the body and the gauge needles swing as the engine warms up; a body without gauges lights up instead. Tap the tail and the nozzle glows and puffs a little smoke.

How do I change the background?

The app opens in space, with planets and satellites drifting slowly behind the rocket. Tap the round button in the top right corner to switch to plain white for this visit. The button below it makes the whole rocket take the colors of the part you change; tap it again to give every part its own colors. The app remembers the color choice.

What are the gold rockets?

Now and then a rocket turns gold, about one in fifty. Press the button and it launches and lands as a gold rocket. Gold rockets go into the hangar: tap the rocket badge in the bottom left corner to open it, and tap a rocket there to bring it back. The hangar holds 12 gold rockets; the badge counts them.

What is the medal?

When the 12th gold rocket is found, all twelve take off from a launch ramp and a medal appears. The hangar then empties and the next round starts. The app remembers the medals on the device.

What is the short guide?

On the first visit of each day the app shows a short guide: swipe each part, tap a part, press the button. The first touch closes it.

How do I turn the sounds off?

Open Parents, answer the grown-up question and switch Sounds off. The app has sound effects and a countdown voice made in the app, and no music; the sounds follow the device volume.

Can the launch be calmer?

Yes. When Reduce Motion is turned on in the device settings, the rocket does not shake, it fades out at lift-off and back in when it lands, and a single copy crosses the sky.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything is included in the app, and the app makes no network connections.

Are there extra purchases?

No. One upfront purchase includes every rocket part. There are no ads, subscriptions, locked packs or in-app purchases.

Why does a question appear?

The Parents section asks a grown-up to answer a multiplication question.

Does the app collect data?

No. Lunamix Rockets collects no data and shares no data. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no analytics, and it makes no network connections. On Android it requests no permissions. See the privacy policy for details.
