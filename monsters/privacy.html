Lunamix Monsters Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026.

About this policy

Lunamix Monsters is provided by Tolga Yeniyurt. This policy explains the information handled when you use the app or contact us for support.

Information in the app

Lunamix Monsters does not require an account. The app does not send gameplay, identifiers or creations to a developer-operated server and contains no advertising or analytics service. It does not request your name, birth date, location, camera, microphone or contacts.

The artwork and background music are included with the app and can be used offline. The music button does not stream from a third-party music service. The last monster you made, the background and the wings choice are remembered on your device only, so they are still there the next time you open the app.

Saving and sharing creations

When you choose Save on iPhone or iPad, the app requests permission to add a generated picture to Photos. It does not request permission to browse or read your existing library. On supported Android devices, Save adds the generated picture to the device's shared Pictures/Lunamix Monsters collection. The app does not scan or read other pictures on the device.

When you choose Share, the app temporarily writes the generated picture to its local cache and opens the system share sheet. You choose the destination. The receiving app or service handles the picture under its own privacy practices. The device manages temporary cached files; they are not uploaded to us.

Ratings

Only in the Parents section, after a grown-up has saved or shared a picture, the app may ask iPhone or iPad to show Apple's standard rating window; Apple decides whether it appears and handles any rating. On Android, a link to the app's Google Play page appears there instead; tapping it opens Google Play. The app itself does not collect ratings or send any data.

Purchases

Apple or Google handles the store purchase. Lunamix Monsters does not collect payment card or bank details and does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Children

Lunamix Monsters is intended for children and families. The app does not request personal information from children or operate accounts, chat or a public creation feed. A fresh grown-up question protects Save, Share and the Parents section. Parents should handle support requests and supervise sharing to other services.

Support

If a parent or guardian contacts tolgayeniyurt@gmail.com, the email address and message are used only to respond to the request. Do not include personal information about a child.

Contact and updates

For questions about privacy, contact tolgayeniyurt@gmail.com. Publisher: Tolga Yeniyurt.
